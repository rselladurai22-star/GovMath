# Figtree (400, 500, 600, 700) and Hanken Grotesk (600, 700, 800)

The site's type since the SumAtlas design system (November 2026), chosen as the
closest open-licence matches to the reference design's body and heading fonts
(the reference's own fonts are commercial or from its CDN, so they are not
used). Figtree by Erik Kennedy and Hanken Grotesk by Alfredo Marco Pradil, both
from `@fontsource` v5.3.0, Latin subset (11 to 14 KB a weight), SIL Open Font
License 1.1 (https://openfontlicense.org). Self-hosted: nothing loads from
Google Fonts.

# Inter Regular (400), Medium (500), Semibold (600), Bold (700) and Black (900)

Inter 4 by Rasmus Andersson, from the `@fontsource/inter` package (v5.1.0,
Latin subset, about 24 KB a weight), licensed under the SIL Open Font
License 1.1 (https://openfontlicense.org). All five weights are self-hosted,
so no stylesheet or font loads from Google Fonts. Inter replaced Lato in
October 2026 for clearer text at small sizes. Kept as a fallback in the font
stack; Figtree replaced it in November 2026.

# Source Sans 3 (400, 500, 600, 700)

By Adobe (Paul D. Hunt), from `@fontsource/source-sans-3` v5.1.0, Latin subset
(about 16 KB a weight), SIL Open Font License 1.1. Used only by the "indus"
calculator look (the US personal loan calculator trial, October 2026).
