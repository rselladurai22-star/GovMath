# Cabinet Grotesk (500, 700, 800): headings and figures

The reference design's heading font, chosen by the owner (November 2026). By
Indian Type Foundry, downloaded unmodified from Fontshare
(api.fontshare.com, about 20 KB a weight) and self-hosted. Licensed under the
ITF Free Font License 2.0, which allows personal and commercial use in any
media and says: "You may self-host the Font Software on your own servers or
infrastructure for use on your own websites and applications, including
through standard webfont technologies such as CSS @font-face."
(https://www.fontshare.com/licenses/itf-ffl). Do not subset or convert the
files: the licence counts modified font files as derivative work.

# Figtree (400, 500, 600, 700): text

The closest open-licence match to the reference's text font (Proxima Nova,
which is commercial). By Erik Kennedy, from `@fontsource/figtree` v5.3.0,
Latin subset (about 11 KB a weight), SIL Open Font License 1.1
(https://openfontlicense.org). Self-hosted.

# Inter Regular (400), Medium (500), Semibold (600), Bold (700) and Black (900)

Inter 4 by Rasmus Andersson, from the `@fontsource/inter` package (v5.1.0,
Latin subset, about 24 KB a weight), licensed under the SIL Open Font
License 1.1 (https://openfontlicense.org). All five weights are self-hosted,
so no stylesheet or font loads from Google Fonts. Inter replaced Lato in
October 2026 for clearer text at small sizes. Kept as a fallback in the font
stack; Figtree replaced it in November 2026.

# Source Sans 3 (400, 500, 600, 700)

By Adobe (Paul D. Hunt), from `@fontsource/source-sans-3` v5.1.0, Latin subset
(about 16 KB a weight), SIL Open Font License 1.1. Used only by the "indus"
calculator look (the US personal loan calculator trial, October 2026).
