# Inter Regular (400), Medium (500), Semibold (600), Bold (700) and Black (900)

Inter 4 by Rasmus Andersson, from the `@fontsource/inter` package (v5.1.0,
Latin subset, about 24 KB a weight), licensed under the SIL Open Font
License 1.1 (https://openfontlicense.org). All five weights are self-hosted,
so no stylesheet or font loads from Google Fonts. Inter replaced Lato in
October 2026 for clearer text at small sizes.

# Source Sans 3 (400, 500, 600, 700)

By Adobe (Paul D. Hunt), from `@fontsource/source-sans-3` v5.1.0, Latin subset
(about 16 KB a weight), SIL Open Font License 1.1. Used only by the "indus"
calculator look (the US personal loan calculator trial, October 2026).

# Manrope (400, 500, 600, 700)

By Mikhail Sharanda, from `@fontsource/manrope` v5.3.0, Latin subset, SIL
Open Font License 1.1. Added 19 October 2026 for a site-wide typography trial
(owner's spec: Manrope everywhere; see the "Manrope trial" block at the end of
govmath-theme.css).
